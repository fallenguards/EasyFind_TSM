# Changelog

## [1.0.1] - 2026-11-23

- Marked up to date for WoW 12.1.7, so the game no longer lists it as out of date there.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-18

- Search your TradeSkillMaster groups and the items in them from EasyFind's search bar, by name or a short form (bs, jc, lw, mats). `@tsm` lists your groups and crafting queue.
- Every item in EasyFind's results shows the TSM price you pick, not only TSM's own items; where that price has none, the vendor price shows, dimmed, and its tooltip says why.
- An item in one of your groups names its group under its name.
- Your crafting queue in the search: type `craft` for each craft with its count and materials.
- The queue's materials as a shopping list in the Notes extension; Notes' shopping lists show TSM prices, and a line Ctrl+clicked at TSM's auction window is searched there.
- Click a group to list its items; click an item to link it in chat, Ctrl+click to try it on, Alt+click to list its group.
- Pick the price on the extension's settings page or with `/eftsm source`, any TSM custom price included.
- Retail, WoW Forever, Mists Classic, TBC Anniversary and Classic Era.
