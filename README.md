# EasyFind: TradeSkillMaster Extension

Your TradeSkillMaster groups, prices and item counts in EasyFind's search bar.

## Requirements

- EasyFind 4.1.0 or later
- TradeSkillMaster

Retail, Mists Classic, TBC Anniversary and Classic Era.

## Features

- Search your TSM groups and the items in them by name. Type `@tsm` to list your groups and crafting queue.
- The TSM price you pick beside every item in EasyFind's results, not only TSM's: your bags, boss loot, the item catalog.
- Hover an item for its price and how many you own: your bags, bank, mail and auctions, then your other characters, guild banks and the warband bank.
- Your crafting queue in the search (type `craft`), each craft with the number queued and its materials, what you have against what you need.
- Click a group to list its items. Click an item to link it in chat, Ctrl+click to try it on, Alt+click to list its group. Right-click any item in one of your TSM groups and pick Show its TSM group.
- Pick the price on the extension's page under Options > Extensions, or type `/eftsm source` and any TSM custom price.
- Switches in EasyFind's filter menu, under Extensions > TSM: prices on item rows, owned counts in tooltips, crafting queue rows.

Auction prices need the data TradeSkillMaster's desktop app keeps up to date; vendor prices and crafting cost work in game alone.
