# EasyFind: TradeSkillMaster Extension

Your TradeSkillMaster groups, prices and crafting queue in EasyFind's search bar.

## Requirements

- EasyFind 5.0.0 or later
- TradeSkillMaster
- The EasyFind Notes extension, for the shopping list

Retail, WoW Forever, Mists Classic, TBC Anniversary and Classic Era.

## Features

- Search your TSM groups and the items in them by name, or a group by a short form (bs, jc, lw, mats). Type `@tsm` to list your groups and crafting queue.
- The TSM price you pick beside every item in EasyFind's results, not only TSM's: your bags, boss loot, the item catalog. Where that price has none, the vendor price shows, dimmed, and its tooltip says why.
- An item in one of your groups names its group under its name.
- Your crafting queue in the search (type `craft`), each craft with the number queued and its materials, what you have against what you need.
- The queue's materials as a shopping list in the Notes extension, ticked off as your bags fill. Notes' shopping lists show TSM prices, and Ctrl+click a line at TSM's auction window to search it there.
- Click a group to list its items. Click an item to link it in chat, Ctrl+click to try it on, Alt+click to list its group. Right-click any item in one of your TSM groups and pick Show its TSM group.
- Pick the price on the extension's page under Options > Extensions, or type `/eftsm source` and any TSM custom price.
- Switches in EasyFind's filter menu, under Extensions > TSM: prices on item rows, crafting queue rows.

Auction prices need the data TradeSkillMaster's desktop app keeps up to date; vendor prices and crafting cost work in game alone.
